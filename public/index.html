Why Appbrew has two blanks

Their pricing isn't published and their Stay Ai depth is a vendor claim we haven't tested, so both are shown as unknown rather than guessed. Its total is scored out of five rows, not seven, and isn't directly comparable until those land.

The two Tapcart paths aren't alternatives. They're phases.

Nothing in the current MVP needs a custom block — watch the counter on the board, which tells you the moment that stops being true.

Custom blocks buy the health and personalisation features parked in future phases: dosing tracker, consumption‑paced reminders, a bespoke subscriber home.

Launch standard, measure against a held‑out control cohort, and spend custom‑block money only where the data shows the standard experience falling short.

Nine things to get in writing

Everything in the matrix assumes Tapcart Enterprise. Most of these could change the arithmetic, and none were answered in Tapcart's last reply.

  1. Which plan includes the Stay Ai integration, and does a success fee apply at that plan?Their reply described the integration repeatedly without naming a tier, and the pricing section came through empty. Your Klaviyo integration page names Enterprise outright — the Stay Ai material names no tier at all. Get the tier and the fee structure confirmed in one email.
  2. Does push send from Klaviyo, or from your push engine?Now the sharpest question of the nine. Your integration page credits the automation to prebuilt automated flows on Tapcart's side that connect to Klaviyo's profiles, segments and flows — which reads as Tapcart sending, using Klaviyo's data. A parallel push engine fails the requirement even if it integrates with Klaviyo. Yes or no, in writing.
  3. The exact list of events and properties that reach Klaviyo, and whether they're segmentable and triggerable.Named events landing as metrics, in real time. Ask for the schema, not a logo.
  4. Can lifecycle deploy in-app modals, banners and embedded cards without an app release, and is that native or a custom block?If it needs custom blocks, the phasing argument changes.
  5. Is the Stay Ai portal native UI or a webview inside the app?“Embedded natively” leaves this open, and they offered to find screenshots rather than sending them.
  6. Who maintains custom blocks through platform updates, and does Enterprise support cover them?Decides whether custom blocks are an asset or a liability we own.
  7. Does “managed Apple & Google developer portal” mean they administer accounts we own, or hold them?The lock-in question. We need the accounts, the listing and the push tokens.
  8. Appbrew: fixed pricing in writing, and a live Stay Ai demo on our own catalogue.Fills the two blanks in the matrix, and it's the only pricing leverage we have.
  9. Two reference customers on Stay Ai at similar revenue, on the phone.Kaged and Credo are Tapcart's chosen references. Ask specifically for a Stay Ai brand.

One thing to settle internally

Success metric is subscriber churn in Stay Ai cohort data, not app revenue in Branch. Branch attributes deep‑linked conversions but not organic app opens, so it structurally undercounts app‑driven revenue. Churn on a held‑out cohort is the only clean read, and it needs defining before launch.

And one about ourselves

Bespoke scores worst on time to launch and dev burden for a reason: G Fuel migrated off a custom app over a developer bottleneck, and Your Super refused a 6–8 month build. Whether that applies to us depends on whether we have in‑house mobile capacity or would be signing an agency retainer.

Business case behind this. 95,753 active subscriptions losing roughly 14% a month; 91% of Shopify revenue subscription‑linked; 93% of sessions on mobile.
